## One-liner

My app helps 13-year-olds find simple recipes using foods they have for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and dessert.

## User + problem

A 13-year-old needs easy meal ideas, clear steps, and a way to find recipes by cuisine or food at home.

## Musts + done when

- Must 1: Browse meals, cuisines, and fridge ingredients
  - Done when: I can see Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Dessert sections, filter by meal, cuisine (including an Asian group), and dietary needs such as vegetarian or gluten-free, show lighter choices, and filter recipes by foods I have.
- Must 2: Search recipes and foods
  - Done when: I can search the names, ingredients, or possible substitutes in 114 distinct recipes, with at least two tofu, beef, and chicken recipes in each cuisine.
- Must 3: Read a recipe, make a shopping list, and find helpful links
  - Done when: I can open a recipe to see its ingredients, possible substitutes, cooking steps, and estimated nutrition, check off ingredients in a list saved on this device, and follow its YouTube search link.
  - I can choose to use my location to open nearby supermarkets in Google Maps. The app does not save my location.
- Keep my filters and fridge foods on this device
  - Done when: My meal and cuisine filters, lighter-choice setting, and fridge foods stay saved after I refresh the page, while search text clears.

## Later: do not build

- Accounts, social sharing, or food delivery
